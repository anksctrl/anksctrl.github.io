HOW THE ARTEFACTS CONNECT

From business case to control system.

  1. Business Case establishes why the investment should be considered.
  2. Charter turns that justification into authorised project scope and governance.
  3. WBS + Schedule translate deliverables into sequenced work.
  4. Risk + Quality define how uncertainty and acceptance are controlled.
  5. Phase Gates create explicit decision points before further investment.
