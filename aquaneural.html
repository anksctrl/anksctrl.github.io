01 / THE CHALLENGE

From operational friction to a scalable control system.

The business case identifies three connected weaknesses in enterprise-scale aquaculture: limited human oversight across many ponds, blind or fixed-schedule feeding, and unreliable manual data.

01Unscalable oversight
02Systemic feeding failure
03Data unreliability
02 / SOLUTION

Sense. Decide. Act. Report.

  • Sense: Sensors monitor dissolved oxygen, pH, temperature and ammonia.
  • Decide: Edge computing and an enterprise dashboard process signals and surface alerts and trends.
  • Act: Aerators, dosing pumps and automated feeders support rapid intervention.
  • Report: Timestamped data supports visibility and investor-ready reporting.
