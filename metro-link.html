03 / SIMULATION • PROJECT AUTOPSY

Metro-Link

Smart Ticketing Overhaul — a distressed-project autopsy focused on root cause, EVM, governance and recovery recommendations.

BASELINE$10M • 12 months
AT MONTH 960% scope delivered
ACTUAL COST$9.5M
SCHEDULE4 months behind
01 / DIAGNOSIS

Five failures became one crisis.

  • Scope creep: facial recognition was added informally without formal change control.
  • Organisational constraint: the PM had little formal authority in a functional structure.
  • Critical path: a six-week IT delay cascaded into a four-month schedule slip.
  • WBS/cost weakness: per-station permit fees were omitted from the baseline.
  • Governance gap: no formal PMO and weak change/risk discipline.

Quantifying the distress.

The EVM analysis shows the project was simultaneously over budget and behind schedule, turning a narrative of “we're low on money” into measurable performance evidence.

03 / WHAT THIS DEMONSTRATES

Recovery thinking.

The report separates immediate crisis actions from structural reforms, with recommendations aimed at restoring control and preventing recurrence. The exercise demonstrates root-cause analysis, integrated change control, schedule dependency thinking, cost-performance analysis and governance awareness.
