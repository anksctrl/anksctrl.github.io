03 / SIMULATION • HEALTH IT

True Vine Hospital

Paper-to-EMR migration using ClinikEHR, structured as a full implementation and transition project.

CLIENTTrue Vine Hospital, Lagos
FACILITY10-bed • ~15 staff
PLATFORMClinikEHR
PROJECTPaper-to-EMR Migration
01 / WHY CHANGE

From paper records to a modern EMR.

The project is designed to improve operational efficiency, support regulatory compliance and deliver better patient care by migrating from paper records to an Electronic Medical Records system.

17+Years serving Lagos
10Bed facility
~15Staff
02 / DELIVERY APPROACH

Six phases from discovery to support.

PHASE 1

Discovery

Assess records, IT needs and regulatory requirements.

PHASE 2

Infrastructure

Prepare hardware, power, internet and environment.

PHASE 3

Configuration

Configure and test ClinikEHR workflows.

PHASE 4

Training

Train staff before go-live.

PHASE 5

Data Migration

Digitize historical patient records.

PHASE 6

Go-Live

Switch to EMR with post-launch support.

03 / KEY DECISIONS

Scope clarity before execution.

  • Historical records: Decide whether to migrate all existing records or begin fresh with new patients.
  • Budget: Establish the available budget range.
  • Timeline: Identify regulatory or accreditation deadlines.
  • Internal champion: Appoint an internal point person.

Project-management takeaway: Scope, dependencies, infrastructure readiness, training, migration and changeover must be considered together in a health IT implementation.
