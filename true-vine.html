04 / SIMULATION • HEALTH IT

True Vine Hospital

Paper-to-EMR migration — a simulated healthcare IT project designed around a 10-bed Lagos hospital moving to ClinikEHR.

CLIENTTrue Vine Hospital
SIZE10 beds • ~15 staff
PLATFORMClinikEHR
DRIVERSEfficiency • compliance
01 / DELIVERY APPROACH

Six phases from discovery to support.

01

Discovery & Planning

Assess records, IT needs, regulatory requirements, scope and budget.

02

Infrastructure Setup

Prepare hardware, power and internet environment.

03

System Configuration

Configure and test ClinikEHR against workflows.

04

Staff Training

Train staff before go-live.

05

Data Migration

Digitise historical patient records.

06

Go-Live & Support

Switch from paper and provide dedicated post-launch support.

02 / KEY DECISIONS

Scope decisions before implementation.

  • Historical records: migrate all existing records or begin with new patients?
  • Budget: determine the available range before locking infrastructure and implementation options.
  • Timeline: identify regulatory or accreditation deadlines driving urgency.
  • Internal champion: nominate the person responsible for internal coordination.
