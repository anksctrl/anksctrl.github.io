02 / ACADEMIC CAPSTONE

Project Sentinel-AI

Enterprise AI Adoption & Governance Platform — a TS Academy capstone case study built around a fictional Nigerian banking group.

STATUSIn progress / Not yet submitted
DOMAINBanking • AI • Governance
APPROACHHybrid predictive / agile
PMODirective PMO
01 / BUSINESS PROBLEM

Bring enterprise AI adoption under control.

The capstone describes a fictional Tier-1 Nigerian banking and financial services group where departments have begun using generative AI tools informally. The case frames three issues: data-governance exposure, inconsistent/unmeasured productivity gains and competitive pressure.

4Priority business units
15 moTarget delivery window
20%Target reduction in average task/query resolution time in initial pilot units
02 / SCOPE

A governance-led transformation.

The plan covers a centralized AI gateway and model registry, data governance and security, integrations with core banking/CRM/fraud/HR systems, formal AI governance and model-risk policies, pilot deployment across four business units, enterprise training and a permanent AI Center of Excellence.

03 / PM FRAMEWORK

Predictive structure with adaptive delivery.

The capstone includes a charter, scope baseline, schedule plan, cost baseline, stakeholder engagement and communication planning, team management, risk management, quality management, change control, governance, PMO structure and development approach.
